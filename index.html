⚡ Zepto Deals & Product Sorter

Extract, filter, and sort Zepto discounts directly in your browser with zero lag.

Method 1: Automatic Userscript (Recommended)

Auto-runs whenever you open zepto.com. A handy floating deals button appears automatically on both PC and Mobile!

Install Userscript (.user.js)

Method 2: Browser Bookmarklet

Drag the green button to your bookmarks bar on Desktop, or copy the code on Mobile.

⚡ Zepto Deals
📱 How to setup on Mobile (Android & iOS)
1
Android: Install Kiwi Browser or Firefox Mobile from the Play Store (they support desktop browser extensions).
2
Install the Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey extension inside the browser.
3
Tap the Install Userscript button above and tap Confirm Install.
4
Open zepto.com — the purple Deals button will automatically appear in the bottom corner!
💻 How to setup on PC (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox)
1
Option A (Userscript): Install Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey, then click the purple button above.
2
Option B (Bookmark): Ensure your bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B), then drag the green ⚡ Zepto Deals button into your bookmarks bar.
